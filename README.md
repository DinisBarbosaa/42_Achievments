🏆 42 Achievements

Turn your 42 journey into shareable achievement cards.

Every submission, every eval, every comeback after a failed project — captured as a card you can share, not just a number on Intra.

💡 What is this?

42's Intra tracks everything you do — but it doesn't tell a story. 42 Achievements connects to your Intra account and turns your real activity into clean, shareable cards:

✅ Passed a project → a card worth showing off
❌ Failed an eval → a motivational card, because the comeback matters too
📈 Leveled up → a milestone card for your progress

Cards are private by default. You choose what becomes public — nothing about a struggle or a low grade is ever shared without you deciding to share it.

Built for 42 Porto, as the seed of a bigger idea: a shared space for 42 students — and eventually alumni — across every campus.
